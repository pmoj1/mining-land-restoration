# Public closure input search — 22 October 2026

Scope: public Perseus sources, targeted searches for native closure workbooks, survey data and revision records. Download manifest records ten inspected files. Search absence is not proof no public copy exists.

## Found
- Yaoure February 2018 conceptual closure plan: PDF pages 52–54 reproduce Appendix 33a cost estimate worksheet. Footprint areas, soil quantities, unit rates, task costs and assumptions are present. Native Excel and original formulas not supplied. Historical conceptual scope, not current approved closure model. Page 50 undiscounted total USD4,091,620. Pages49–50 explicitly exclude uncertain long-term water management/treatment costs.
- Public sustainability XLSX data books FY2022–FY2026 downloaded and read. Closure and Biodiversity & Environment tabs contain annual disclosures, financial provisions, land disturbance and rehabilitation figures. Land data inspected are company totals and country/region breakdowns, not surveyed spatial boundaries or task-level models. FY2026 includes retrospective FY2021–FY2026 columns. Definitions, restatements and coverage require reconciliation before comparisons.
- Nyanzaga2025 technical report printed323–324 identifies Mine Earth2022 closure cost estimate, GIS/aerial inputs and rates database. It explicitly explains adjustment for changed surface disturbance and replacement TSF estimate by Knight Piesold2024. Do not allege unadjusted 2022 basis. Table and narrative figures should be queried with owner before treating any discrepancy as an error.
- Edikan2019 geotechnical report printed29 references topography dated21Feb2019, hydrographic survey5–7June2018, DTM model and a life-of-mine tonnage Excel attachment to22May2019 email. Source files are referenced, not delivered as raw attachments.

## Not found in inspected sources
- Native engineering closure workbook with formulas and linked quantity/rate inputs.
- Original raw survey point clouds, GIS disturbance polygons and corresponding two-date snapshots.
- Internal closure workbook revision/approval history or change log.
Five inspected PDFs have no embedded files according to pypdf attachment inventory. This does not imply there are no detailed tables or drawings in their pages.

## Demonstration and validation
Public data can demonstrate evidence extraction, reconstruction of historical PDF cost schedule and published document comparison. Label reconstructed formulas and historical scope. Cannot establish current model omissions, current liability adequacy, time savings or willingness to pay.
Request one site, two approved model versions, corresponding dated survey/disturbance exports, rate assumptions and approved change record, ideally anonymised. Human closure engineer validates findings.
